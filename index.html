Our Story

Made by hand, the way it's always been.

We hope that when you purchase our pierogies, you do not feel like you have entered a store, but more so feel as if you've entered our home. — The Helena's Family

We are a family owned and operated business dedicated to a homemade product that exemplifies quality in its best form. Starting from a recipe passed down in our Ukrainian and Polish family, we opened our doors in 1995 to bring a traditional European comfort food into American homes — and we've been rolling, filling, and frying pierogies (plus selling our kielbasa) ever since.

Every part of the process is done by hand: the dough is mixed and rolled, the onions are caramelized for our sauce, the potatoes are peeled for our filling, and every pierogi is hand-pinched closed — the same way it's always been done in our kitchen.

We do our best to accommodate you and your orders, because we value each and every customer who walks through our door.

God Bless. — Смачного! Smacznego!
