How it all began

A recipe worth sharing.

Some of the best memories happen around the kitchen table.

We are a family owned and operated business dedicated to a homemade product of the highest quality. Starting from a recipe passed down in our Ukrainian and Polish family, we opened our doors in 1995 to bring traditional European comfort food into American homes.

Every step is done by hand: the dough is rolled, the onions are caramelized for our sauce, and the potatoes are peeled for our filling.

We hope that when you buy our pierogies, you don't feel like you've walked into a store, but into our home. We do our best to accommodate you and your orders, because we value every customer who walks through our door.

God bless. Смачного! Smacznego!
