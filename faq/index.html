Frequently asked questions.

Don't see your question? Just give us a call.

Call (845) 626-2958
Can I order online?

Not at this time. Please call us at (845) 626-2958 or order in person at the shop. We don’t take orders by email or Facebook.

Do you ship?

Sorry, we don’t ship at this time.

How are pierogies sold?

All pierogies are sold by the dozen. Our meats are sold by the pound.

Is every flavor available every day?

Availability varies by day, and seasonal fruit pierogies are only available in season. Call ahead if you have your heart set on a particular flavor.

How much notice do you need for a big order?

Large orders need one week’s notice. Holiday orders need 2–3 weeks.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash and local checks.

When are you open?

Thursday through Sunday, 12 to 5 PM. In bad weather, please call or check our Facebook page for updated hours.
